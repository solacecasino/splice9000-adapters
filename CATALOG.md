# splice9000 adapter catalog

Add-ons for [splice9000](https://github.com/solacecasino/splice9000). Pick one, download the zip for your
computer, then **drop it on the splice9000 window**. splice9000 shows what an adapter can do and
asks before installing it. Remove it any time from the Adapters panel.

## Audio

### Loudness Normalize

Export formats that level the sound to the loudness YouTube, Spotify, podcasts and broadcast expect (−14, −16 and −23 LUFS).

[**Download** (every platform)](https://github.com/solacecasino/splice9000-adapters/releases/download/v0.2.0/loudness-normalize-0.1.0.zip)

Can nothing beyond its own commands, panels and formats. Adds 3 export formats. v0.1.0 · by splice9000 · MIT · [source and help](https://github.com/solacecasino/splice9000-adapters/tree/main/loudness-normalize) · updated 2026-10-28

### Scene Split

Cuts a clip wherever the picture changes scene, using ffmpeg's scene detection. One undo step.

### Silence Cut

Removes the silent stretches from a clip (jump cuts for talking video), leaving a little room around words. One undo step.

**Download:** [Mac (Apple Silicon)](https://github.com/solacecasino/splice9000-adapters/releases/download/v0.2.0/silence-cut-0.1.0-macos-arm64.zip) · [Mac (Intel)](https://github.com/solacecasino/splice9000-adapters/releases/download/v0.2.0/silence-cut-0.1.0-macos-x64.zip) · [Windows](https://github.com/solacecasino/splice9000-adapters/releases/download/v0.2.0/silence-cut-0.1.0-windows-x64.zip) · [Windows on Arm](https://github.com/solacecasino/splice9000-adapters/releases/download/v0.2.0/silence-cut-0.1.0-windows-arm64.zip) · [Linux](https://github.com/solacecasino/splice9000-adapters/releases/download/v0.2.0/silence-cut-0.1.0-linux-x64.zip) · [Linux on Arm](https://github.com/solacecasino/splice9000-adapters/releases/download/v0.2.0/silence-cut-0.1.0-linux-arm64.zip)
